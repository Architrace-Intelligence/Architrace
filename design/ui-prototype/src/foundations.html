Design foundations · MVP

Calm control room

Dark by default for long sessions beside terminals and dashboards, with a complete light theme. One cool accent for interaction, four hues reserved for node types, red and amber reserved for severity and drift. IBM Plex Sans for text, JetBrains Mono for identifiers and numbers. Dense where data lives, generous where decisions are made.
